Hermes

A personal household automation assistant.

Hermes is a private, single-user project run by one person on their own computer. It reads that person’s own email and files to answer everyday family questions — what is on the school lunch menu, when the next day off is, what a school newsletter actually said.

It is not a product, not a service, and is not offered to anyone else. There are no accounts to create and nothing to sign up for.

What it connects to

  • Gmail — reads school email, and archives it once summarized.
  • Google Drive — reads menu and calendar files shared with the account.

Both are the owner’s own account. Hermes has no ability to reach anyone else’s data.
