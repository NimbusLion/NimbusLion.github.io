Privacy Policy

Hermes — personal household automation

Hermes is used by one person: its owner. It accesses only that person’s own Google account, after they grant it permission.

What is accessed

  • Gmail (gmail.readonly, gmail.modify) — to read school email and to archive or mark it read once it has been summarized.
  • Google Drive (drive.readonly) — to read school menu and calendar documents.
  • Basic profile and email address — to confirm which account signed in.

Where the data goes

Nowhere. Everything is processed and stored on the owner’s own computer. Summaries are generated by a language model running locally on that machine and are delivered to the owner’s private family chat.

  • No data is sent to any third-party service.
  • No data is sold, shared, transferred, or used for advertising.
  • No analytics, tracking, or profiling of any kind.
  • No other person can use this application or reach the data it holds.

Retention and deletion

Cached email summaries and menu files are kept locally only while useful and can be deleted at any time by removing them from the machine. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately and permanently ends all access.

Limited Use

Hermes’ use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
